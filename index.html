Featured: App Stability Overhaul

NBFC & employee-management apps · Vexil Infotech

Situation

Production apps were running at roughly 60% crash-free sessions, with frequent freezes affecting daily users.

What I did

  • Diagnosed root causes from crash and ANR data (Firebase Crashlytics, Play Console Android Vitals).
  • Fixed the highest-impact crashes and removed main-thread work that was causing freezes.
  • Used AI-assisted tooling (Gemini) to speed up tracing and drafting fixes, while reviewing and owning every change.

Result

  • Crash-free sessions: ~60% → 99.7%
  • Crash-free users: 99.8%
  • Frozen frames: reduced by 60% week over week
